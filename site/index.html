FAQ

What is Peekhole?

In Leonida, Peekhole is where creators get paid by the fans who can't stop peeking. Out here, it's a fan-made meme coin and community inspired by the GTA VI trailer, specifically the Make It Rain Mondays poster on the club wall.

Is this an official Rockstar project?

No. Peekhole is an independent fan project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Rockstar Games, Take-Two Interactive, or anyone who works on GTA VI.

Can I use $PEEK inside GTA VI?

No. $PEEK is a meme coin on Solana with no utility in GTA VI or any other game. The club, the dancers and Vice City are fiction. The coin is real, and its price moves.

Where does the QR code go?

The QR codes on Peekhole posters point to this page. The poster in the official trailer belongs to Rockstar, and its QR code goes to Rockstar's own site.

How do I avoid fake $PEEK tokens?

Copy the contract address from this page only. Admins never DM first, never ask for your recovery phrase, and never offer to "sync" or "validate" your wallet.

Is this financial advice?

No. $PEEK has no intrinsic value and no promise of returns. Meme coins can go to zero. Only spend what you'd happily throw at a stage.
