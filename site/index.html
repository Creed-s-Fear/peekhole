FAQ

What is Peekhole?

Vice City's premiere creator tipping platform. Every Monday night at Jack of Hearts, the DJ drops the lights, the creators take the stage, and the crowd makes it rain in Peek Cash. Think of it as the intersection of a nightclub and a tip jar — only louder, and with better lighting.

What is Peek Cash?

It's the currency used on Peekhole — think of it as chips at the casino. You load up through Maze Bank, you tip, the creators cash out. It's the only way to make it rain at Jack of Hearts.

How do Make It Rain Mondays work?

Every Monday at 10 PM Vice City time, the DJ calls it. The lights drop, the creators take the stage, and everyone tips at once. The whole room lights up. It's a weekly ritual — miss one and you'll hear about it.

Where is Jack of Hearts?

1987 Ocean Drive, Vice Beach, Leonida. Look for the heart-shaped neon sign above the door. If you found the QR code, you already know where to look.

Can I become a creator?

Auditions every Thursday night. Bring your stage name, your moves, and an iFruit device. No previous experience required — but it helps. Ask for Dani at the bar.

Is my account private?

Your Peekhole profile is anonymous by default. Creators see your display name, not your Maze Bank details. What happens at Jack of Hearts stays at Jack of Hearts.

How do I get in?

Walk in any Monday after 10. The bouncer checks your age, not your net worth. VIP access is earned — top tippers get the invite. Reservations: 555-PEEK.
