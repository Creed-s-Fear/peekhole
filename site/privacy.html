Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

1. Overview

Peekhole Entertainment ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the Peekhole platform ("Service"). What happens at Jack of Hearts stays at Jack of Hearts — and we mean it.

2. Information We Collect

Information you provide

  • Account information: Display name, email address, and age verification status
  • Transaction data: Peek Cash purchase history, tip history, and account balance
  • Communications: Messages you send to support or through the platform

Information collected automatically

  • Device information: Browser type, operating system, device model
  • Usage data: Pages visited, features used, session duration
  • Log data: IP address, access times, referring URLs

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Operate and maintain the Service
  • Process Peek Cash transactions through Maze Bank Digital
  • Enforce our Terms of Service and Content Guidelines
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access
  • Communicate service updates and announcements
  • Improve the platform experience

4. What We Don't Do

  • We do not sell your personal information to third parties
  • We do not share your tip history with other users
  • We do not display your real name or Maze Bank details to creators
  • We do not store payment card numbers — all payments are handled by Maze Bank Digital

5. Anonymous Profiles

Your Peekhole profile is anonymous by default. Creators see your display name only. Your Maze Bank account details, transaction history, and personal information are never visible to other users or creators. You can change your display name at any time.

6. Data Sharing

We may share your information only in the following circumstances:

  • Maze Bank Digital: Transaction processing and fraud prevention
  • Legal requirements: When required by law, subpoena, or court order
  • Safety: To protect the rights, safety, or property of our users, creators, or the public
  • Service providers: Trusted partners who assist in operating the platform, bound by confidentiality agreements

7. Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your information, including encryption in transit, access controls, and regular security audits. However, no system is completely secure. We encourage you to use a strong, unique password for your Peekhole account.

8. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. Transaction records are retained for seven years as required by Leonida financial regulations. You may request deletion of your account at any time by contacting us at 555-PEEK.

9. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Opt out of promotional communications

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@peekhole.vc or call 555-PEEK.

10. Cookies

We use essential cookies to maintain your session and remember your preferences. We do not use third-party tracking cookies or advertising cookies. Your browser settings can be configured to block cookies, but some features of the Service may not function properly.

11. Age Restriction

The Service is intended for individuals 18 years of age and older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If we learn that we have collected information from a minor, we will delete it immediately.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. Your continued use of the Service constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
