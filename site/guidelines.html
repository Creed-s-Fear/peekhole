Content Guidelines

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Peekhole is a stage, not a free-for-all. These guidelines keep the room safe, the creators respected, and the Monday nights legendary. Break them and the bouncer handles the rest.

For Members

Respect

  • Treat creators like professionals. They're performing for you — act like the room deserves it.
  • No harassment, hate speech, threats, or discrimination of any kind.
  • No unsolicited personal questions, real-name guessing, or doxxing attempts.
  • No stalking creators across platforms. What happens on Peekhole stays on Peekhole.

Tipping Etiquette

  • Tips are voluntary. Do not pressure other members to tip.
  • Do not request refunds for tips. All tips are final.
  • Do not use tips to demand specific actions from creators outside their stated boundaries.
  • Peek Cash only. No offering real-world goods, services, or meetings in exchange for content.

Privacy

  • No screenshots, recordings, or screen captures of any content on the platform.
  • No sharing, reposting, or redistribution of creator content anywhere.
  • VIP content is strictly confidential. Leaking VIP content results in permanent ban.
  • Do not share other members' display names, tip amounts, or activity outside the platform.

For Creators

Content Standards

  • All performers must be 18 years of age or older.
  • Content must comply with all applicable laws of Vice City and the State of Leonida.
  • No content involving minors, non-consensual activity, or illegal substances.
  • Creators are responsible for ensuring all individuals appearing in their content have given consent.

Professionalism

  • Maintain your profile accurately. No impersonating other creators or public figures.
  • Do not solicit members for off-platform transactions or meetings.
  • Respond to reports and platform inquiries within 48 hours.
  • Adhere to your posted schedule. Repeated no-shows may result in lineup removal.

Earnings

  • Peek Cash tips are credited to your creator account in real time.
  • Cashout is processed through Maze Bank Digital on a weekly cycle.
  • Creators are responsible for their own tax obligations in Leonida.

Prohibited Content

The following is not permitted anywhere on the platform:

  • Content depicting or promoting violence, self-harm, or dangerous activities
  • Hate speech targeting race, ethnicity, gender, sexuality, religion, or disability
  • Spam, scams, phishing, or fraudulent schemes
  • Impersonation of Peekhole staff — real staff wear the heart pin and never contact you outside the app
  • Advertising or promotion of competing platforms
  • Any content that violates the intellectual property rights of others

Enforcement

We take violations seriously. Depending on severity:

  1. Warning: First offense for minor violations. You'll hear from us.
  2. Temporary suspension: Repeated or moderate violations. 7 to 30 days.
  3. Permanent ban: Severe violations, harassment, or VIP content leaks. No appeal, no refund.

All enforcement decisions are at the sole discretion of Peekhole Entertainment. If you believe an action was taken in error, contact us within 14 days.

Reporting

See something that breaks these guidelines? Report it:

  • Use the in-app report button on any profile or content
  • Email: safety@peekhole.vc
  • Phone: 555-PEEK (ask for the safety team)

All reports are reviewed within 24 hours. Reporter identity is always kept confidential.
